## Settlement boundary and an observed mismatch

WaterX's current 5m market page says the beginning reference is the price at the beginning of the titled range, and settlement is the **Chainlink BTC/USD TWAP over that range**; Up wins at equality. It cites the Chainlink BTC/USD TWAP 60s stream as its resolution source. Its live chart is a WaterX Oracle preview that can differ from settlement. This app's Coinbase feed is an independent comparison, never a replacement for the settlement target. Provider-reported resolution is checked for exact historical ID and closing epoch before acceptance (`server/waterx/source.ts`, `server/waterx/service.ts`); independent Chainlink verification of every outcome is **not established**.

One recurring **development** 5m settlement mismatch was inspected rather than silently accepted. The saved row ending at `1790732100` has round ID `3c8a1377-...` and an unconfirmed provenance-only observation with a $68,123.45 anchor; WaterX's historical response for the same closing epoch has ID `f2fd486e-...` and an $83,417.80 anchor. The saved ID also appears in a local source-parser test fixture. It remains unresolved; neither that row nor the identity guard was changed. Its precise insertion path is not proved. This example does not establish that every pending settlement has the same cause.
