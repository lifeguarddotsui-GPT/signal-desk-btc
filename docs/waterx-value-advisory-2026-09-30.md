# WaterX probability-and-value advisory — source-only review

This change adds a **read-only** round-matched advisory response at `/api/waterx/advisory?interval=5|15&amount=5`. It derives both market probabilities and side-price arithmetic from one current WaterX live payload, including provider/market/interval/round/start/expiry identity. The UI consumes that one response for its displayed market probabilities and indicative amounts; amount edits refetch it and stale data is withheld while pending. Valid side prices do not depend on an unrelated reference arriving first.

The observed WaterX page calls its amount field **USD** and offers a `+$5` button, but the public side-price feed does not verify executable $5 sizing, fee inclusion, slippage, void/refund rules, or the cutoff for manual entry. The quote timestamp is when this app observed the public response, not a provider-issued executable quote time; its actual expiration is unknown. The calculator therefore labels the entered $5 as an indicative spend **before unknown fees**, shows hypothetical gross receipt and before-fee break-even per side, and does **not** compute actual total cost, net profit, or expected net value. The assumption of $1 per winning share and zero losing payout is disclosed, not asserted as a verified fill. A separate pure function implements the verified binary formula for later use only after all-in costs and fee-adjusted receipts can actually be verified.

The state machine's only reachable states with current evidence are OBSERVE, UNAVAILABLE, LOCKED and EXPIRED. A high market-derived probability can never activate FAVORABLE. WAIT, TOO LATE and FAVORABLE require a qualified model, verified quote/cost/cutoff, and a forward-tested uncertainty buffer that do not exist. Source code alone does not establish live trading value.

Production inspection before publishing still showed 15m last valid collection at 13:40:07 UTC while 5m advanced. The per-interval collection watchdog now retries if a recursive timer disappears without launching a second in-flight read. The exact reason the prior 15m timer stopped is not proved; a genuinely stuck in-flight database operation still requires a process supervisor. Production autoscale is not an always-on worker.

The production history API exposed exact withheld label reasons: at inspection, 21 of 67 retained 5m rows and 1 of 38 retained 15m rows were withheld because **“Final confirmed WaterX anchor is missing or contradicts the frozen prospective anchor.”** This evidence is retained, not relabeled from Coinbase or force-accepted. Whether a later confirmed anchor can produce a fresh eligible pre-expiry prediction requires separate investigation.

Tests cover indicative arithmetic, unknown fees and net value, missing probabilities, interval mismatch, stale/out-of-window and non-WaterX quotes, locked/expired markets, amount validation, a 99% market probability without a favorable state, and watchdog non-overlap. No production migration or paid hosting change is included. The published app, daily production learning, browser-closed collection, real fill feasibility, statistical validation, and a 72-hour observation remain unverified until independently observed.
