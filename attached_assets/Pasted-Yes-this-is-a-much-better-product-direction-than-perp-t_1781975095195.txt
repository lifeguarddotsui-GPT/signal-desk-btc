Ranking Model

Each market gets a score from 0–10.

Crypto 15m Ranking Formula

Final Score =
30% price momentum
20% distance to target
15% volume confirmation
15% volatility quality
10% trend alignment
10% market mispricing edge

Crypto Signal Logic

For each BTC/ETH/SOL 15m contract:

If current price is above Coinbase target and momentum is rising:
  Favor YES
If current price is below Coinbase target and momentum is falling:
  Favor NO
If price is close to target with low volatility:
  Avoid or low rank
If YES/NO price is too expensive relative to model probability:
  Avoid
If model probability exceeds market implied probability by 8%+:
  Candidate pick

⸻

Sports Ranking Formula

Final Score =
25% external odds edge
20% recent team/player form
15% injury/news advantage
15% market mispricing
10% volume/liquidity
10% time-to-event quality
5% narrative/sentiment

Sports Signal Logic

Compare Coinbase implied probability to external sportsbook odds.
If Coinbase says 42% but sportsbook consensus implies 52%:
  Edge = +10%
  Candidate pick
If news/injuries support the side:
  Increase score
If market is illiquid:
  Reduce score
If event is too far away:
  Reduce urgency
If confidence < 6.5:
  Do not alert

⸻

Risk Management Rules

The app must show recommended bet sizing.

Score 9–10: max 3% bankroll
Score 8–8.9: max 2% bankroll
Score 7–7.9: max 1%
Score 6–6.9: watchlist only
Below 6: no bet

Add this warning everywhere:

Prediction markets are risky. Contracts can expire worthless. This app provides research and ranking only, not guaranteed profit.

Coinbase itself warns that prediction contracts involve substantial risk and may result in losing the entire investment.
