3) Chain sync worker (the real fix)

3.1 Fetch sources

6) Fix PnL tracking (revise calculation method)

6.1 Compute Equity (single source of truth)

At render time (or backend aggregation), compute:

equity =
  agent_wallet_balance_sui
+ sum_over_open_positions(collateral + unrealized_pnl)
+ reward_wallet_balance_sui

6.2 Compute Net Deposits

net_deposited = deposits - withdrawals

6.3 Compute Total PnL

total_pnl = equity - net_deposited - protocol_fees - gas_costs

6.4 Realized PnL

Only from CLOSED/LIQUIDATED events:
	•	CLOSED:
	•	realized = close_payout - open_collateral_locked - fees (and optionally funding)
	•	LIQUIDATED:
	•	realized = -open_collateral_locked (minus any returned dust)

If you don’t have payout/return events, you must derive them from the event’s numeric fields or balance deltas within that tx digest only.

Important: “Collateral moved into a vault” on OPEN is not a loss. It becomes “locked collateral”.
