This is almost always one of these:

If your tx references the wrong oracle feed (or wrong market object), Typus checks oracle for market X while your order is for market Y → mismatch.

3) Your acceptable price is computed from the UI price (or float math)

Even if you attach oracle, if you derive acceptablePrice from a non-oracle source or bad scaling, it can still mismatch.
